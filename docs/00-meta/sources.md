## Risk sizing (retail guidance, not regulation)

Retail options risk writeups commonly discuss ~1–2% of equity max loss per defined-risk trade and tighter aggregate “heat” bands (often ~10–15%). Treat those as starting points to compare against your own written policy — not as advice.

## How to re-verify

1. Prefer primary docs (OCC, broker permission pages, CRA guidance) over blogs.  
2. For any new numeric “system rule,” require ≥2 independents **or** label it `[operator preference]`.  
3. Re-check broker TFSA/options matrices before acting — they change.
